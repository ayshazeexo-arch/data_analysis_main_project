# Superstore Sales Analysis – Business Questions

## 1. Project Background
The objective of this project is to analyse retail sales data to understand business performance, profitability, customer behaviour and product performance.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Investigate returned orders.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 3. Key Business Questions

### A. Sales Performance
1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. Which months generate the highest and lowest sales?
3. How do sales change year over year?
4. Which regions contribute the most revenue?

### B. Profitability Analysis
5. What is the total profit and profit margin?
6. Which products generate the highest profit?
7. Which products generate losses?
8. How do discounts affect profitability?

### C. Customer Analysis
9. Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
10. Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
11. Which customers contribute the most profit?

### D. Product Analysis
12. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
13. Which sub-categories are the most profitable?
14. Which products have the highest sales volume?

### E. Regional and Returns Analysis
15. Which region generates the highest sales and profit?
16. Which regional manager oversees each region?
17. What percentage of orders are returned?
18. Which regions have the highest return rates?
