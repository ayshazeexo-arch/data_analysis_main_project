However, Home Office has the highest profit margin at 14.02%, indicating stronger profitability relative to its sales. --- The analysis shows a strong association between higher discount levels and lower profitability. Transactions with discounts of 0%, 10% and 20% generated positive total profits, while discount levels from 30% to 80% generated losses. The business should review its discount strategy, particularly at higher discount levels.

--- The overall order return rate is 5.79%, indicating that a relatively small proportion of orders were returned. Further analysis by product category and region could help identify areas where return rates may be higher.
